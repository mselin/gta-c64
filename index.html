SLEAZEBURG 64

A top-down crime satire in the style of an 8-bit home computer game: 320×200 pixels, 16 colours, sprites and three-voice sound. Walk, drive, take jobs from the flashing J markers, dodge the law, and raise $25,000 to buy the election. Get arrested with nothing in your pocket and the game is over.

Most jobs point you to where you need to go. One does not: for the cult of 6-7 you have to count streets. Avenues are numbered from the west and streets from the north, starting at 1, and your current crossing is shown next to the timer.

An original game. Every place, business and official in Sleazeburg is made up.

There is also a version for a real Commodore 64, being written in 6502 assembly. So far you can drive around the city: play it in the browser or download it. Its source code is on GitHub.

CONTROLS

Arrows / WASD
Walk, drive
Touch screen
Hold a finger on the screen and you walk, or drive, towards it. In a car, the further away the finger, the faster you go; hold it behind the car to reverse, or on the car to brake. The buttons fire, get in and out of cars and work the handbrake.
Space
Fire or punch on foot, horn in a car
X / Shift
Handbrake, for sliding round corners
E / Enter
Get in or out of a car
M
Music on or off (starts off)
R / N
Next radio station / next tune
F
Sound effects on or off
Z
Scale: how big the city is drawn beside its people. 1× as big as cars, 2× (the default) closer to life, 3× true to scale
P
Pause
I
Open or close this page
Esc
Back to the title screen, to just listen to the radio. Your game is kept.

RADIO

The radio plays on the title screen and while you drive. Tunes are real Commodore 64 music, streamed on demand from the High Voltage SID Collection and played in your browser by the same Commodore 64 emulator the HVSC website uses.

DISCORD RICH PRESENCE

Optional, and off unless you turn it on. It shows what you are doing in the game, such as "Loitering in Gentrifia", on your Discord profile, where anyone who can see your profile can see it. It needs a small helper program that you run on your own computer. What is sent, and to whom.
